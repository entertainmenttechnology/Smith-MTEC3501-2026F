### ☐ 6. Watch: Project Scales — NSV, LVP, PoC

**Time:** 15–20 minutes  
**When:** During class session  
**Status:** Required video

Watch the instructional video introducing project scales.

**What to understand:**

**North Star Vision (NSV)**  
The broadest, most ambitious version of your project—what it could ultimately become.

**Least Viable Product (LVP)**  
The minimum meaningful version that retains the project's essential purpose.

**Proof of Concept (PoC)**  
The focused version you will develop during MTEC 3501 to test feasibility and complexity.

**Note:** You are not finalizing these scales during Week 1! Just recognize that your project can exist at multiple scales.

When done, move to the next task.
