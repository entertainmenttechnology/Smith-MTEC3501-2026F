## Week 1 Lesson Plan — Asynchronous Course Launch

> **Status:** Working Draft  
> **Mode:** Asynchronous online  
> **Course Time Model:** 3 instructional hours + approximately 6 independent study hours per week  
> **Week 2:** Face-to-face class  
> **Development Branch:** `development`

---

## Week 1 Overview

The first week introduces students to MTEC 3501 through a guided asynchronous sequence of short instructor videos, existing instructional media, readings, forms, account setup, and project-development activities.

Students are encouraged to begin the sequence during the regularly scheduled class period, using that time as a focused launch session. They may continue independently throughout the week. All required Week 1 onboarding and developmental activities should be complete before the Week 2 face-to-face class.

The Week 1 experience is primarily independent, but students establish an early communication channel through Discord and later begin interacting with classmates around emerging project ideas.

The week has two distinct expectations:

### Rigid Infrastructure Expectations

Technical onboarding tasks should be completed fully and correctly.

Students should establish working access to:

- GitHub
- their individual MTEC 3501 repository
- VS Code/local repository workflow
- Zotero
- Discord
- required Google resources

### Flexible Developmental Expectations

Creative and project-development work should remain exploratory and provisional.

Students are **not** expected to finalize their Culmination Project during Week 1.

The class will remain primarily within the **Speculate** phase during the opening weeks, while allowing exploratory research to inform and complicate speculation.

## 9. Project Scale: North Star Vision, LVP, and PoC

**Approximate time:** 15–20 minutes  
**Pathway:** Short instructional video + reference material  
**Required**

Introduce three different scales of project thinking.

### North Star Vision — NSV

The broadest intellectual, creative, technical, cultural, and experiential universe of the project.

### Least Viable Product — LVP

The minimum meaningful realization of the project required to retain its essential purpose.

### Proof of Concept — PoC

The focused demonstration developed during MTEC 3501 to investigate feasibility, complexity, technical challenges, and readiness to proceed into ENT 4501.

Students are **not expected to finalize these definitions during Week 1.**

At this stage they should begin recognizing that a project can exist at multiple scales.

### Conceptual Distinction

**SRDMPA describes how a project develops.**

**NSV / LVP / PoC describe the scale or realization of the project being considered.**

# Approximate Week 1 Workload

The required Week 1 sequence is expected to take approximately:

**5–7 hours for most students**

Additional time may be required for:

- account problems
- GitHub/VS Code troubleshooting
- Zotero setup problems
- exploratory research
- peer interaction
- students unfamiliar with Git workflows

This workload fits within the course expectation of approximately:

**3 instructional hours + 6 independent study hours per week**

The Week 1 sequence should not attempt to fill all nine available hours artificially. Remaining time provides necessary flexibility for troubleshooting, variation in prior technical experience, and additional speculative development.

---

# Week 1 Design Principle

The first week should produce two outcomes simultaneously:

## Infrastructure

Students establish a reliable professional project-development environment.

## Speculative Space

Students begin expanding their project thinking without prematurely committing to a final proposal.

The intended Week 2 condition is:

> **Your infrastructure should now work. Your project should not yet be settled.**
