# Instructor Planning Notes — Week 8 Asynchronous PoC Development

**Instructor reference only; do not include in the student-facing assignment.**

- This is independent asynchronous work for Week 8. The Week 9 class meeting is the submission deadline and discussion point, not an additional work period for completing the assignment.
- Students have completed the mid-semester presentations. The assignment should ask them to consider oral and written panel feedback, review formative repository feedback, and decide how each affects their project.
- The PoC is the target for the rest of the semester, culminating in the final presentation. Week 8 asks students to begin practical work with an essential component or process; it does not require a completed or functioning PoC.
- Build on the Week 4 Issues workflow and the Week 5 distinctions among PoC, LVP, and North Star Vision. Link to those materials rather than repeating or changing their requirements.
- Keep the repository indicators formative. Do not present them as a grade, score, or formal consequence.
- Keep guided activities independent and untimed. Do not imply synchronous attendance or monitoring.
