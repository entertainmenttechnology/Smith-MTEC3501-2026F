# MTEC 3501: Final Presentation and PoC Demonstration Deliverables

**Related document:** [Midterm Project Presentation and Deliverables](02-Presentation_Deliverables.md)

## Purpose

The final presentation takes place in **Week 15**. Students present and demonstrate the Proof of Concept (PoC) developed during MTEC 3501, explain what they learned from building and testing it, and show how that evidence informs the next-stage project: the ENT 4501 Least Viable Product (LVP).

The PoC is the final making outcome for MTEC 3501. It is a focused, functional demonstration, not the complete LVP and not the full North Star Vision (NSV).

## Scope: From PoC to North Star Vision

Explain how the three scopes relate:

1. **MTEC 3501 Proof of Concept (PoC):** The focused system or experience demonstrated this semester. It should test an important technical, design, or conceptual assumption.
2. **ENT 4501 Least Viable Product (LVP):** The minimum complete project planned for the next course. Explain how the PoC informs its design and what work remains.
3. **North Star Vision (NSV):** The larger aspirational direction that gives context to the LVP. It is not a required build for either presentation.

## Required Presentation and Demonstration

Prepare a **10-minute presentation** that includes or is coordinated with a demonstration of the PoC. Use the demonstration, recorded evidence, or other appropriate media to make the PoC's behavior and results clear. Follow any additional presentation and Q&A logistics announced for the class session.

The presentation should cover:

1. **Project and scope:** State the project's purpose and distinguish the demonstrated PoC from the planned LVP and aspirational NSV.
2. **Research and design:** Summarize the most relevant research, precedents, and design decisions that informed the PoC.
3. **PoC demonstration:** Show the PoC's core behavior or experience and identify the specific question, assumption, or capability it tests.
4. **Technology and process:** Explain the systems and tools used, important implementation choices, and how the work developed from the midterm plan.
5. **Evidence and reflection:** Describe tests or observations, what worked and what did not, current limitations, and what the student would change or investigate next.
6. **Path to the LVP:** Identify the next design or production steps, remaining competencies to develop, and how the PoC informs the ENT 4501 LVP plan.

## Suggested Slide Sequence

This is an adaptable example, not a required template. Students may combine, divide, or reorder slides, but the deck and demonstration should collectively address all required content. Keep the presentation and demonstration within the 10-minute limit.

| Slide | Include | Suggested time |
|---|---|---:|
| 1. Project and presenter | Student/team name, working title, project form, and a clickable GitHub repository link or QR code. | 0:30 |
| 2. Project and scope | Specific project description; intended audience/experience; concise distinction among the PoC, next-course LVP, and North Star Vision. | 1:00 |
| 3. Research and design decisions | Most relevant research or precedents and the design choices they informed. Cite sources on the slide or in a reference slide. | 1:00 |
| 4. PoC question and demonstration | State what the PoC tests, then show its core behavior or experience live or with prepared media. | 3:00 |
| 5. System and implementation | Show the system or process, technologies used, important implementation choices, and changes made since the midterm plan. | 1:30 |
| 6. Evidence, limits, and learning | Summarize tests or observations, what worked, limitations, and what the student learned or would change. | 1:30 |
| 7. From PoC to LVP | Explain what the PoC contributes to the planned ENT 4501 LVP, what remains to be done, and the next steps. | 1:30 |

Students may place the repository link on the title or closing slide. The course [Student Record](../Student_Record.md) also maintains individual repository links; verify that the record is current if relying on it for panel access.

## Required Project Materials

Make the final project record discoverable from the README in the student's project repository. It should include or link to:

- Presentation slides and any media needed to understand the demonstration.
- A visible, clickable GitHub repository link on the title or closing slide, or a current repository link in the course Student Record.
- The functioning PoC or, when the work cannot be run in the presentation setting, a clear recorded demonstration and explanation of the limitation.
- Current project proposal and the LVP design/planning documentation for ENT 4501.
- Relevant system/design documentation, test evidence, and reflection on the PoC's results and limitations.
- Research bibliography and Zotero collection, with sources cited in project documentation and presentation materials as appropriate.
- Updated GitHub issues, milestones, and project roadmap showing completed work and next steps.
- An AI Collaboration Statement in an appropriate project artifact when AI was used, consistent with the syllabus policy.
- A final self-assessment describing progress, feedback considered, and plans for continued development.

The PoC need not be a polished or complete LVP. It should be sufficiently functional and documented to show what it tests and what was learned. If a live demonstration is unreliable, prepare a recorded backup and be clear about what the recording does and does not establish.

## Evaluation Criteria

- **PoC demonstration and evidence:** The core behavior is shown, and the evidence supports claims about what the PoC tests.
- **Project understanding and scope:** PoC, LVP, and NSV are distinguished and coherently related.
- **Research and design reasoning:** Relevant sources and decisions are connected to the work.
- **Technical and creative execution:** The chosen methods are explained, including constraints and limitations.
- **Iteration and reflection:** The student interprets results, feedback, and changes from the midterm plan.
- **Communication and documentation:** The presentation is clear, within the time limit, and supported by an organized project record.
- **Next-stage planning:** The relationship between the PoC and ENT 4501 LVP is actionable and specific.

## Final Check

Before presenting, verify that the repository README links to the presentation, PoC evidence, project documentation, research, and next-step plan. The final presentation should communicate both what the PoC demonstrates now and what remains to be developed in ENT 4501.
